02 / WHO WE ARE

Small studio.
Big ideas.

CiphAnon is built around making things instead of just talking about them.

CiphAnon Studios is an independent creative studio focused on building games, websites, scripts and experimental projects.

Some projects are polished. Some start as completely ridiculous ideas. That's part of the fun.

The goal is simple: build things that are fun to use, interesting to look at and worth coming back to.
